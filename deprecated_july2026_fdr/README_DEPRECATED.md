# Deprecated: July 2026 FDR material

The files in this folder were produced in July 2026 during the first revision
and were superseded before resubmission. They are kept only so that the
repository history is complete. **They are not part of the published analysis
and must not be cited or reused.**

Why they were withdrawn:

- `ANALYSIS_REPORT_FDR_CORRECTION.md` and the `Table_S2..S4_*_FDR.csv` tables
  apply a Benjamini-Hochberg correction to `1 - PIP`. A posterior inclusion
  probability is not a frequentist p-value, so the procedure is not a valid
  false-discovery-rate correction. The paper reports PIP-threshold sensitivity
  and posterior expected false-discovery proportions instead
  (`results/audit/pip_threshold_sensitivity.csv`,
  `results/audit/posterior_expected_fdr.csv`).
- The tables were computed from the full-chain PIPs of the first submission
  (iterations 1 to 1,000), not from the post-burn-in PIPs that the paper uses.
- `analysis_scripts/` contains pipeline drafts (permutation testing, an
  "external validation" step) that were never run on real data; any
  external-validation values they reference were placeholders.

The authoritative outputs are in `results/` and are regenerated by
`analysis/04_audit/`.
